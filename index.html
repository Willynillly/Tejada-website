About Us

The Antiquarian Bookstore was built to bridge books and pastries together.
Providing students, workers, lounge seekers alike a place to rest
whilst providing refreshments and books to escape from their troubles for a while.
The idea was made when a group of friends roamed the entire campus for a place to stay and
despite the campus' enormous size we couldn't find a place where we can borrow books,
eat and drink at the same time. Then the idea of the antiquarian store came to life.

Literary and Mystery Hub

Caring for a Weathered Spine

Keep antique books upright, away from direct sun, and handle them with clean, dry hands. Avoid tape, and let a conservator treat loose hinges.

What Does "Foxing" Mean?

Those rusty brown spots on old paper are called foxing. They come from humidity and metal traces in the paper, and they are a common sight in vintage volumes.
